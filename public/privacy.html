Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026

Wavu helps merchants respond to social-media product questions and continue buyer conversations on WhatsApp. It processes comment identifiers and text, social handles, product context, handoff tokens, and order details only to provide this service.

Wavu does not sell personal data. Access tokens are kept outside the source repository. Operational records are restricted to the merchant’s connected Google Sheet and cloud services. Data is retained only as needed for the merchant’s workflow and may be deleted by the merchant.

Connected platforms remain governed by their own privacy policies. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact the Wavu project owner at nestroymusoke22@gmail.com.
